The Rice Travel Group's 1-Day Cu Chi Tunnels & Ho Chi Minh City Discovery Tour takes you on an unforgettable journey beginning at the Cu Chi Tunnels — one of Vietnam's most remarkable and unique military constructions in modern history.

Stretching for more than 200 km underground and dug entirely by hand using rudimentary tools, the tunnel network stands as a vivid testament to the ingenuity, resilience, determination, and indomitable spirit of the Vietnamese people during the war years.

After exploring the meaningful historical stories of Cu Chi, The Rice Travel Group takes you back to the heart of Ho Chi Minh City to experience a completely different side of Saigon — dynamic and modern, yet still rich in distinctive cultural and architectural heritage.

A special highlight of the Ho Chi Minh City experience is a ride on the Saigon WaterBus in the late afternoon. From the river, you will enjoy a completely different perspective of the city, admire the beautiful sunset along the Saigon River, and capture some of the most memorable photos of your trip.

Detailed Tour Itinerary

A harmonious blend of historical depth and modern urban discovery (08:00 AM – 05:30 PM)

1 Day Complete
08:00 AM

Pick-up at 189 De Tham Street, District 1 & Banh Mi Breakfast

District 1 ➔ Cu Chi (~65 mins)

Depart from central Saigon in a comfortable, air-conditioned private 7-seat vehicle. While travelling toward the countryside outskirts, enjoy freshly made crispy Vietnamese banh mi with authentic fillings, freshly prepared by The Rice Travel Group's kitchen team.

09:15 AM

Dai Viet Cu Chi Traditional Lacquerware Workshop

Cultural Craftsmanship Stop

Before entering the historic tunnel terrain, make a gentle stop at an authentic traditional lacquerware workshop. Watch skilled Vietnamese artisans painstakingly craft multi-layered lacquer paintings, mother-of-pearl inlays, hand-embroidered artworks, and natural gemstone jewelry.

10:00 AM

Cu Chi Tunnels Historical Site Exploration

Core Historical Highlight

Our visit is thoughtfully paced to deliver deep historical insight without being rushed:

  • Historical Documentary: Watch an authentic archival film depicting the daily lives, courage, and underground warfare of the Cu Chi guerillas.
  • Hidden Entrances & Forest Camouflage: Discover cleverly disguised trapdoor entrances, wooden test wells, and underground meeting chambers hidden beneath leaf-covered forest floors.
  • Relics & Traps: View an intact US tank wreckage enveloped by dense foliage and inspect ingenious handmade punji bamboo traps.
  • Tunnel Crawling Experience: Experience crawling through actual multi-level underground tunnels, seeing ventilation termite mounds and underground infirmaries.
  • Military Scrap Workshop: See how unexploded battlefield materials were repurposed into survival tools and weapons.
  • Optional Sport Shooting Range: Guests who wish may experience sport target shooting with AK-47 or M16 rifles (optional at personal expense).
12:00 PM

Wartime Tasting: Boiled Cassava & Hot Jasmine Tea

Cu Chi Forest Rest Area

Conclude the underground exploration by sitting together in the shaded jungle pavilion to savor piping-hot boiled cassava dipped in savory peanut-sesame salt, served alongside fragrant hot tea — the exact sustenance that sustained fighters during the most arduous years of the war.

01:00 PM

Return to Ho Chi Minh City & City Exploration Begins

Cu Chi ➔ Central Saigon

Relax in the private vehicle as we journey back into the vibrant heart of Saigon. Take a midday breather with chilled water and refreshing towels before starting the afternoon urban heritage trail.

02:00 PM

Ho Chi Minh City Heritage & Architectural Icons

District 1 Heritage Quarter

Explore the century-old French colonial architectural landmarks of District 1:

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica & Central Post Office: Admire the Gothic arches and Gustave Eiffel-designed vaulted iron ceilings.
  • Nguyen Van Binh Book Street: Stroll through the leafy pedestrian sanctuary dotted with artistic cafés and bookstores.
  • Independence Palace (Reunification Palace): Step inside the iconic presidential landmark that witnessed the historic reunification of Vietnam in 1975.
  • Ben Thanh Market: Immerse in the vibrant pulse of local commerce, aromatic spices, and traditional artisan souvenirs.
04:30 PM

Saigon WaterBus Golden Hour Sunset Cruise

Signature River Experience

Board the modern Saigon WaterBus at Bach Dang Wharf as the afternoon sun softens. Feel the cool river breeze and admire the panoramic skyline of modern skyscrapers reflecting across the water. Our accompanying photographer will capture breathtaking sunset portrait moments.

05:30 PM

Tour Conclusion at District 1 & Farewell

189 De Tham Street

Arrive back at 189 De Tham Street, District 1. Your guide and photographer will bid you farewell. Within 24–48 hours, a link to your high-resolution edited photo gallery will be delivered to your email / WhatsApp.
