## Detailed Four-Stage Itinerary Breakdown

### Stage 1 (07:30 – 10:15 AM): Morning Market & Neighborhood Temple
- **07:30 – 09:00 AM:** Begin at Ben Thanh Market’s East Portal. Indulge in a steaming bowl of [authentic bún riêu](/ben-thanh-market-food-guide), laden with river crab cakes. Stroll around the South Portal clock tower to admire the 1952 Bien Hoa ceramic reliefs in the crisp morning light.
- **09:15 – 10:15 AM:** Walk 200 meters down Truong Dinh Street to **Mariamman Hindu Temple**. Shed your footwear at the threshold, absorb the warmth of incense, and admire the colorful hand-painted deities ascending the tiered Gopuram tower.

### Stage 2 (10:30 AM – 13:45 PM): Fine Arts Museum & Apartment Cafes
- **10:30 AM – 12:00 PM:** Cross Le Thi Hong Gam Street to the **[HCMC Museum of Fine Arts](/hcmc-museum-of-fine-arts-guide) (97A Pho Duc Chinh)**. Spend 90 minutes exploring Hui Bon Hoa’s 99-window estate, marveling at stained glass reflections and viewing the monumental national treasure *Spring Garden of North, Central, and South Vietnam*.
- **12:15 – 13:45 PM:** Savor an authentic lunch featuring fresh spring rolls and claypot dishes near Nguyen Thai Binh Street. Afterwards, ascend the patterned tile stairways of a vintage Pasteur Street apartment block to sip single-origin Vietnamese pour-over coffee in a quiet room.

### Stage 3 (14:00 – 17:45 PM): Independence Palace, Metro Station & Riverfront
- **14:00 – 16:00 PM:** Stroll beneath tree-shaded Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Boulevard to the **Independence Palace**. Unpack master architect Ngo Viet Thu’s Tropical Modernism, decipher Eastern philosophical ideograms (Cat, Khau, Trung), and step into the reinforced subterranean wartime telecommunication bunkers.
- **16:15 – 17:00 PM:** Return to the renovated plaza before Ben Thanh Market. Ride the escalators down into the **2026 [Ben Thanh Central Metro Station](/ben-thanh-central-metro-station-guide)**, gazing up through the massive circular Lotus Toplight skylight as afternoon rays pierce the glass concourse.
- **17:00 – 17:45 PM:** Walk down Le Loi Boulevard past the Saigon Opera House, reaching **Bach Dang Riverfront Park**. Rest along the granite promenade, catching cooling river winds as the sun sets over the Saigon River.

### Stage 4 (18:00 – 20:30 PM): Street Dining & Evening Drinks
- **18:00 – 19:30 PM:** Circle back to Phan Boi Chau Street flanking Ben Thanh Market. Feast on betel-leaf wrapped beef grilled over open charcoal embers and fresh coastal shellfish.
- **19:45 – 20:30 PM:** Conclude your walk at an open-air rooftop lounge overlooking the square, sipping a refreshing cocktail as the illuminated clock tower presides over evening traffic below.
